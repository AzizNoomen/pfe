About KnoGen

KnoGen is a cross-document Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) application that leverages a knowledge graph as its database. It provides an intuitive experience to ingest files, visualize the extracted concepts and relationships from those documents as a graph, and includes a Q&A feature for querying the documents.

KnoGen was developed as part of an internship at Elyadata to obtain my engineering degree, within the framework of the end-of-studies internship for engineering students of the National School of Computer Sciences (ENSI).
